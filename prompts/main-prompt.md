# Main Prompt — Luxe Studio Salon

## Purpose

This prompt was used to generate a premium website concept and website-ready content for a fictional local salon business.

## Prompt

Create a premium, modern, luxury salon website for a fictional brand called Luxe Studio Salon.

The design should feel elegant, minimal, and high-end with a black, white, and gold color palette. Use smooth animations, glassmorphism effects, rounded cards, and a clean premium UI.

Include these sections:

- Hero section with the heading "Where Beauty Meets Luxury"
- CTA button: "Book on WhatsApp"
- About Us section
- Services section
- Price list with sample pricing
- Before & After gallery
- Customer testimonials
- Meet Our Experts section
- Why Choose Us section
- Booking section with WhatsApp integration and appointment form
- Google Maps location placeholder
- Contact information
- Footer with Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp links

Services should include:

- Haircut
- Hair Spa
- Hair Coloring
- Keratin Treatment
- Facial
- Bridal Makeup
- Manicure & Pedicure
- Beard Grooming
- Skincare

Make the website fully responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Optimize the content and structure for local SEO.

Add subtle scroll animations and hover effects.

The website should look like it belongs to a premium salon and should be impressive enough to convince a real salon owner to purchase a similar website.

## Prompting Strategy

The prompt defines:

1. The business type
2. Target brand positioning
3. Visual and communication style
4. Required website sections
5. Services to be promoted
6. Customer conversion goal
7. Local SEO requirement
8. Technical and responsive requirements

This structured approach helps the AI generate content and a website that match the business requirements.
